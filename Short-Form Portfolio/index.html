Course Goals


1. Consider the role of rhetoric, technology, and ethics in acts of making.

Within today's rapid evolution of technology, the use of rhetoric and ethics in the creation of design becomes increasingly prevalent within our daily lives. Artificial intelligence brings about the topic of the integrity of your individual work. As identified by the course syllabus, artificial Intelligence should be utilized as a tool. It is versatile and can be used to do various tasks, but your work should reflect your effort to create a design that embodies your unique perspectives.

2. Leverage Design Principles.

Design principles provides a structure for creating effective and significant designs. These principles should be applied throughout all of your work and be considered to play a crucial role in drawing your audience's attention. These principles have already been explored throughout the course and will continue build upon each other throughout creations. These principles should be directly utilized to improve and enhance your designs. Moving forward, I hope to continue learning and utilizing these principles to deliver nuance to my work.

3. Engage with others in a workshop environment.

Learning to collaborate effectively with others is a crucial aspect of design that allows one to be given a second perspective to refine your own work and be given a chance to learn from their own choices. This allows you to enhance your own design through peer perspectives and feedback. As I have already began doing so, I hope to continue supporting and engaging with my peers for any suggestions they may have to improve my work.



4. Practice revision within and across media forms.

The process of revision is an essential part of design, which allows you to continually improve your own work and learn of new choices that can make your design more effective for viewers. One must make an effort to view their work through different perspectives to ensure the design is effective in communicating its message to the audience.For the remainder of the course, I expect to accept the concept of imperfection to continue refining my designs through feedback and self-reflection.

5. Learn to present your work.

The ability to effectively explain the purpose behind your own creation and process that went into its development is a crucial skill for many professionals in their respective fields. Its importance in design is the emphasis on the designs purpose and function. Designs altogether bring value to the intended audience by communicating their message. I hope to continue developing this skill of not only presenting my work to others, but analyzing its components and being able to explain them.

Course Goals

  1. Consider rhetoric, technology, and ethics

    Throughout these projects, rhetoric, technology, and ethics served as perpetuators of design intent. The Bugatti Altair advertisement illustrates how these elements worked together to support a specific communication goal. Rhetoric shaped the advertisement's persuasive appeal through luxury-focused imagery, branding, and promotional copy designed to position the fictional vehicle as an aspirational product. Technology enabled the realization of that vision through digital design tools, including removing image backgrounds, repositioning vehicle imagery, and compositing visual elements to create a cohesive luxury aesthetic that supported the advertisement's branding and message. Ethics informed decisions regarding audience influence and responsible advertising practices, requiring consideration of how visual and textual elements guide consumer perception. Rather than functioning as separate considerations, rhetoric, technology, and ethics reinforced one another to communicate a cohesive message and strengthen the effectiveness of the final design.

  2. Leverage design principles

    Throughout these projects, design principles served as essential tools for communicating information effectively. The CRISPR infographic demonstrates how hierarchy, spacing, balance, and proximity were used to organize complex scientific content into a format that was approachable for a general audience. Careful consideration was given to the placement of the CRISPR-Cas9 diagram, supporting statistics, and explanatory sections so readers could navigate the information in a logical sequence and focus on key concepts without becoming overwhelmed. By leveraging these principles, the design improved clarity, supported audience comprehension, and strengthened engagement with the topic.

  3. Engage in a workshop environment

    Throughout the development of the accessibility-focused design project, engagement in a workshop environment played a significant role in refining my approach. Feedback from the professor encouraged me to remove text outlines that reduced readability and accessibility, while reviewing my peers' work exposed me to additional considerations for audience needs. Observing a design that used decorative typography with potential readability challenges prompted me to think more critically about how users interact with written content. These observations led me to reevaluate text sizing, alignment, spacing, and overall layout decisions to better support individuals requiring accessibility accommodations. By incorporating both critique and peer observations into the revision process, I improved the clarity and usability of the project while strengthening my understanding of accessibility-centered design and audience-focused communication.

  4. Practice revision across media

    Throughout the development of the Bugatti Altair advertisement, revision played a significant role in refining how the design communicated its intended message. Early versions focused primarily on the vehicle itself, but later revisions introduced a branded header ribbon featuring the Bugatti emblem to place greater emphasis on company identity and strengthen brand recognition. I also added a gradient overlay that enhanced the metallic appearance of the vehicle and better aligned the imagery with the luxury aesthetic conveyed through the advertisement's typography, branding, and promotional copy. Through these revisions, I refined the relationship between branding, text, and visual elements to create a more cohesive and persuasive design.

  5. Learn to present your work

    Presenting my two-page editorial magazine spread allowed me to discuss both the visual and conceptual aspects of the original design. While presenting to the class, I focused on the article's central question, “What if aging were a disease?”, because it explored a relevant and thought-provoking concept. Although the project involved recreating an existing publication layout, I explained how the placement and scale of the word “DISEASE” interacted with the accompanying imagery to create a strong visual focal point and reinforce the article's message. Preserving the impact of these elements while exploring typography, spacing, and layout relationships became a central focus of my work. Presenting the project strengthened my ability to analyze design choices and communicate how typography and composition influence the effectiveness of a visual message.
